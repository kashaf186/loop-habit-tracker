about loop

We got tired of habit apps that felt like a second job.

Loop started as a shared spreadsheet between three students trying to survive finals season. It's still built on the same idea: a habit tracker should take less time than the habit itself.

How Loop got here

  1. Spring 2024

    A spreadsheet, three names, one column of checkmarks

    Started as a shared Google Sheet to keep each other honest during a brutal exam block.

  2. Fall 2024

    First working prototype

    Rebuilt the spreadsheet as a tiny web app so friends outside the group chat could join in.

  3. Summer 2025

    1,000 streaks kept

    Word spread across a few campuses; the calendar view became the whole product.

  4. Today

    Still three people, still one column of checkmarks

    Loop grew, but the rule stayed the same: if a feature doesn't help you show up tomorrow, it doesn't ship.
